About ChalzTracker

Your Personal AI Usage Tracker

100% standalone multi-account quota monitor, smart account router, and desktop tray widget.

Unlimited Accounts · Zero External CLI Required

Sign in manually to as many Personal, Work, or Team accounts as you own across Antigravity, OpenAI Codex, Claude, Cursor, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Kimi Code, xAI Grok, Z.AI, and MiniMax.

  1. ★ Smart Account Router & Burn-Rate Pace Automatically scores every account by bottleneck headroom and expiring-window urgency ("Use before reset!"), while forecasting whether your burn rate is on-pace or running hot.
  2. Desktop Widget, Mini Button & System Tray Alerts Press W for the two-pane desktop widget, M for the compact mini usage pill, or minimize to the Windows System Tray with native 80% / 95% & reset notifications.
  3. Private Local Vault (`~/.chalztracker`) OAuth tokens and keys never leave your machine except to query official provider usage endpoints directly.
